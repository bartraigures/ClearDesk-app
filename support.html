Support

What do I need?

  • Your own self-hosted osTicket, reachable over https. Tested releases are listed on the home page.
  • An agent (staff) account. Customer accounts cannot sign in.

I can't sign in

  • Enter the address of your helpdesk, for example support.example.com. The app adds https:// and finds the agent panel itself.
  • Plain http addresses are refused.
  • Check the same username and password work in a browser at your helpdesk's /scp page. Several failed attempts can lock the account for a while; that is your helpdesk's setting.
  • Single sign-on (OAuth, SAML) and authenticator-app codes are not supported. Emailed verification codes are.

It keeps asking for a verification code

With two-factor sign-in, your helpdesk asks for a new emailed code whenever its session has to be renewed. How long a session lasts is set by your helpdesk's administrator.

The Sort button offers different choices on different queues

The app shows the sorts your helpdesk offers for that queue. Some queues allow sorting by column; others only offer the helpdesk's named sorts, such as "Priority + Due Date".

Did the customer get an email?

  • Reply and Resolve, Email customer send your message to the customer.
  • Note, Resolve, No email, and changing status or assignee from the menu do not email the customer.

The dashboard is empty or missing

Your agent account needs permission to see the dashboard in osTicket. Ask your administrator.

A dashboard number doesn't match the list it opens

The dashboard counts events during the period, for example tickets created this week. The list shows tickets as they stand now. The two will not always agree.

My widgets are out of date

Widgets show what the app last saved, with the time. Opening the app refreshes them. iOS decides when background refreshes run.

How do I get instant notifications?

From version 1.1, with Clear Desk Full. Your helpdesk's administrator first installs the free Clear Desk plugin on the osTicket server; then you switch on Instant notifications in the app's Settings. Without the plugin there are no instant notifications. The notifications guide has every step.

It says "Offline. Showing saved tickets."

The app could not reach your helpdesk and is showing saved copies. You can read them, but replying and other actions need a connection. Tap Retry when you are back online.

Something stopped working after my helpdesk was upgraded

Clear Desk works through the agent panel's pages, and an osTicket upgrade can change them. Please get in touch with your osTicket version and what stopped working.

Subscription

  • Manage or cancel in Settings, your name, Subscriptions.
  • If you have subscribed on another device, tap Restore purchases on the subscription screen.
