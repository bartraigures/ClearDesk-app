Privacy policy

Last updated 15 November 2026.

Clear Desk is an app for iPhone and iPad that lets a support agent work with their own osTicket helpdesk. This page says what happens to your data when you use it.

The short version

Clear Desk has no account with us and no server of ours. We receive nothing from the app: no analytics, no crash reports, no usage data, no identifiers, and no ticket content. Everything goes between your device and the helpdesk address you enter.

What the app stores, and where

  • Your agent password is stored in the Keychain on your device, and is sent only to your helpdesk when signing in.
  • Your helpdesk address and username are stored on your device.
  • Saved tickets for offline reading (queue lists and the tickets you have opened, with a few saved ahead) are stored on your device, in the app's own storage.
  • Widget content (ticket counts and the latest tickets' sender, subject and date) is stored on your device so widgets can show it. It can appear on your Home Screen and Lock Screen.

Signing out in the app deletes the saved password, the saved tickets and the widget content. Deleting the app removes everything.

Network requests

The app connects only to the helpdesk address you enter, over https. It signs in to the agent panel as you would in a browser. Your helpdesk sees what it sees from any agent session, and its administrator's own policies apply to what it records.

Subscriptions

If you subscribe to Clear Desk Full, the purchase is handled by Apple. We do not receive your name, payment details or Apple Account. Apple provides us with sales totals that do not identify you.

What we don't do

  • We don't collect, sell or share personal data.
  • We don't use advertising, analytics or tracking code.
  • The app contains no third-party SDKs that see your data.

Tickets contain other people's data

Tickets on your helpdesk include your customers' names, addresses and messages. Clear Desk keeps copies on your device as described above. Use the app's lock, and your organisation's own rules for devices that hold customer data.

Children

Clear Desk is a work tool, is not directed at children, and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If the app changes in a way that affects this policy, this page will be updated and the date above will change.
